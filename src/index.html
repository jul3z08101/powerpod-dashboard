About the Power Pod

The Power Pod is a 3-session peer accountability program for women in BWF&O. You set one career or skills-based goal, work toward it, and your pod holds you accountable.

This year we are focused on goals that move the needle at IBM: skilling up, visible projects, certifications, and measurable work outcomes.

No rankings. No judgment. Progress over perfection, always.

How to Use the Dashboard

1. Click My Progress at the top of the page.

2. Select your pod name, then your name.

3. Fill in your goal details under Session 1.

4. Click Save — your progress is stored instantly.

Return any time to update your status, add notes, or complete Session 2 and 3 fields.

What Makes a Good Goal

Your goal should be SMART: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound. Focus on skills, projects, certifications, and measurable work outcomes that get you to the next level at IBM.

Example: By November 12, I will complete the IBM AI Fundamentals badge and present one key takeaway to my pod.

Do
  • Grow a skill tied to your role (e.g., advanced Excel modeling by Nov. 15)
  • Lead or contribute to a visible work project or process improvement
  • Expand your professional network: 3 informational chats this quarter
  • Earn a certification or complete a training relevant to your career path
  • Improve a measurable work outcome (close cycle, reporting accuracy, turnaround time)
Not this
  • Personal fitness or wellness targets (steps, gym, weight)
  • Financial or budgeting goals outside of work
  • Hobbies, travel plans, or side projects unrelated to your role
  • Family, relationship, or household commitments
  • Anything you would not be comfortable sharing publicly with the pod
How Progress is Tracked

Your engagement score updates as soon as you fill in Session 1 — no need to wait for Session 2. The pod progress bars and the "Leading the Way" section on the overview page reflect real activity from day one.
